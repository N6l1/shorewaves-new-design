About Shore Waves

Three decades
of trust.

From a perfume house in Al Khobar to a Kingdom-wide chemical supplier — a journey built on trust, safety and service.

Our story

Built on quality and customer care.

Shore Waves Trading Company was founded in 1989 by Mr. Saad Al-Shehri in the perfume industry in Al Khobar, Eastern Province. For over two decades the company earned its reputation on quality and customer care.

In 2014, responding to growing demand from Saudi industry, Shore Waves expanded into chemicals and trading. Today we are headquartered in Dhahran, import from suppliers around the globe, and serve clients across the entire Kingdom.

Infrastructure

Our facilities.

Purpose-built for the safe storage and handling of industrial chemicals.

  1. Closed warehouse equipped with modern fire suppression systems for the safe storage of chemicals.
  2. Dangerous Goods (DG) and non-DG chemicals stored in strictly separated areas.
  3. 24/7 CCTV coverage across the entire facility for security and accountability.
