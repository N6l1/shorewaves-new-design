About Shore Waves

From a perfume house in Al Khobar to a Kingdom-wide chemical supplier — a journey built on trust, safety, and service.

Our Story

Built on Three Decades of Trust

Shore Waves Trading Est was founded in 1989 by Mr. Saad Al-Shehri in the perfume industry in Al Khobar, Eastern Province, Saudi Arabia. For over two decades the company earned its reputation on quality and customer care.

In 2016, responding to the growing demand from Saudi industry, Shore Waves expanded into the chemical and trading business. Today we are headquartered in Dhahran, import chemicals from suppliers around the globe, and serve clients across the entire Kingdom.

Infrastructure

Our Facilities

Purpose-built for the safe storage and handling of industrial chemicals.

  • Closed warehouse equipped with modern fire suppression systems for the safe storage of chemicals.
  • Dangerous Goods (DG) and Non-DG chemicals are stored in strictly separated areas.
  • 24/7 CCTV coverage across the entire facility for security and accountability.
  • Modern communication systems and infrastructure enabling near zero-complaint service.
