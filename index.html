Who we are

A legacy of trust, since 1989.

Shore Waves traces its roots to 1989, when founder Mr. Saad Al-Shehri began in the perfume industry in Al Khobar. In 2014, answering growing industrial demand, the company moved into chemicals and trading.

Today, from our base in Dhahran, we import chemicals from around the globe and serve 100+ clients across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
