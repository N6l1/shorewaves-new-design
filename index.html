Who We Are

A Legacy of Trust Since 1989

Shore Waves Trading Est traces its roots back to 1989, when founder Mr. Saad Al-Shehri began in the perfume industry in Al Khobar, Eastern Province. In 2016, responding to growing industrial demand, the company expanded into the chemical and trading business.

Today, headquartered in Dhahran, we import chemicals from around the globe and proudly serve 100+ satisfied clients across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
